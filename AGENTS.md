# AGENTS.md

Instructions for coding agents (Codex and others) working on this repo.

1. Read and follow `CLAUDE.md`. Its rules apply to every agent: permanent URLs, never rewrite
   dates, Jordan's writing is his, no edits in `themes/PaperMod/`, never push to `main`.
2. The redesign plan, its status and the decisions already made are in `docs/redesign/PLAN.md`.
   The approved design is `docs/redesign/mockups.html` (open it in a browser; "Combined homepage"
   and "Article page" tabs).
3. Do one plan PR at a time on its own branch, verify it on its Netlify deploy preview, and open a
   pull request. Do not merge.
4. Build and checks: see `README.md` and the "Checks before opening a PR" section of `CLAUDE.md`.
